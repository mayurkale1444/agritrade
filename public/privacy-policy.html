1. Introduction & Overview

AgriTrade ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of our users — including farmers, wholesale traders, commission agents (आडत), and agricultural market participants across India.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the AgriTrade mobile application (Package: in.agritrade.app) and related services. This policy complies with the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, Google Play's User Data Policy, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act, 2023), and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

3. How Your Information is Stored & Handled

AgriTrade uses an on-device storage architecture (HTML5 Local Storage) to maintain your preferences without requiring cloud registration:

  • Language Preferences: Your selected app language (Marathi, Hindi, English, Tamil, or Kannada).
  • Trader Role & Preferences: Your trade role (Trader, Farmer, Commission Agent, Wholesaler) and selected primary APMC market.
  • Favorite Watchlist: APMC mandis and commodities you choose to track.
  • Calculation History: Figures calculated in the Net Profit Calculator (purchase price, sale price, quantity, freight, loading/unloading, mandi cess) are stored strictly on your phone so you can reopen past trades.

4. Device Permissions Requested

AgriTrade requests only minimal, necessary device permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to query daily verified mandi commodity rates and arrival figures from the Government of India AGMARKNET database.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / CAMERA (Optional): Required only if a trader chooses to take a picture of a physical mandi rate bulletin poster to extract rates. If not used, this permission is never invoked.

5. Official Government Data Sources & Disclaimers

Official daily APMC mandi modal prices, arrival volumes, and minimum/maximum rates are sourced transparently from the Directorate of Marketing & Inspection (AGMARKNET), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India, through the Open Government Data (OGD) platform at data.gov.in.

Disclaimer: AgriTrade is an independent agricultural intelligence utility and is not an official government entity. Government data is presented as reported by respective APMC market secretaries under the National Data Sharing and Accessibility Policy (NDSAP).

6. User Rights & Complete Data Deletion (One-Tap In-App)

Permanent Profile & Data Deletion Instructions

In compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Requirement, users have complete control to wipe their data at any time without restrictions:

  1. Open the AgriTrade application.
  2. Tap on the More (सेटिंग्ज) tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Profile (प्रोफाइल हटवा).
  4. Confirm by tapping Yes, Delete Profile & Clear Data.

Result: All your stored preferences, name, age, selected APMC markets, watchlist bookmarks, and calculation history are immediately and permanently erased from the device.

Alternatively, you may email us at mayurkale1444@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request" and we will assist you within 24 hours.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The application is tailored for agricultural trade professionals and adult farmers. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.
